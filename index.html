How we build

Less software between you and the thing you came to do.

  1. LightweightA

    Small by choice.

    No feature earns its place by sounding impressive. Each product stays focused on the job it exists to do.

  2. ClearB

    Obvious in use.

    Interfaces explain themselves through structure, language, and familiar actions—not instructions.

  3. RespectfulC

    Your attention is yours.

    No dark patterns, manufactured urgency, or noisy defaults. Useful software knows when to step aside.
